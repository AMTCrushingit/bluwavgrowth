5 Signs Your Business Website Is Costing You Customers

Looking for a high-converting website for your business? A bad website is worse than no website. It tells customers you're not serious. Here are the 5 most common website mistakes modern businesses make, and what a modern, conversion-focused website actually looks like.

BluWav Growth·4 min read·June 28, 2026

Your website is your first impression. Customers check you online before they call. If your website is not working for you, it is working against you. Here are the 5 signs.

What they find in those first few seconds determines whether they become a customer or move on to your competitor.

A great website builds trust, answers questions, and makes it easy to take the next step. A bad website does the opposite, it raises doubts, creates friction, and sends customers away.

Here are the 5 most common signs that your business website is costing you customers, and exactly what to do about each one.

A salon like this one loses customers every day to competitors with better online presence, even when the service is superior.

01

It Doesn't Work Properly on Mobile

Over 80% of internet users browse on their phones. If your website doesn't work perfectly on mobile, text too small, buttons too close together, images that don't scale, you're losing the majority of your visitors before they even read a word.

It Takes Too Long to Load

Internet speeds vary. A website that takes more than 3 seconds to load loses over half its visitors before they see your content. Every extra second costs you customers, and Google penalises slow sites in search rankings too.

There's No Clear Call to Action

Visitors land on your site and don't know what to do next. No "Call Now" button, no WhatsApp link, no booking form. Every page needs one clear, obvious action. Without it, interested customers leave without contacting you.

The Design Looks Outdated

A website that looks like it was built in 2010 tells customers your business isn't serious. First impressions happen in milliseconds, and a dated design signals that you might be out of touch in other ways too.

Google Can't Find It

Your website exists, but Google can't find it because it has no proper page titles, no meta descriptions, and no keywords that match what customers are searching for. A beautiful website that nobody finds is a wasted investment.

What a High-Converting Business Website Looks Like

A website that actually grows your business isn't just about looking good. It loads fast on mobile, makes it immediately clear what you do and who you serve, and gives visitors an obvious next step, whether that's calling, WhatsApp-ing, or booking.

The difference between a website that converts and one that doesn't usually comes down to a handful of specific, fixable issues. BluWav builds mobile-first websites delivered in 48 hours. Most modern business websites we review have at least three of the five problems above, and fixing them doesn't require rebuilding from scratch.
