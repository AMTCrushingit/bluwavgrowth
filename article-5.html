5 Signs Your Caribbean Business Website Is Costing You Customers

A bad website is worse than no website. It tells customers you're not serious. Here are the 5 most common website mistakes Caribbean businesses make — and what a modern, conversion-focused website actually looks like.

BluWav Growth·4 min read·June 28, 2026

Your website is often the first impression a potential customer has of your business. Before they call you, before they visit you, before they WhatsApp you — they Google you.

What they find in those first few seconds determines whether they become a customer or move on to your competitor.

A great website builds trust, answers questions, and makes it easy to take the next step. A bad website does the opposite — it raises doubts, creates friction, and sends customers away.

Here are the 5 most common signs that your Caribbean business website is costing you customers — and exactly what to do about each one.

01

It Doesn't Work Properly on Mobile

Over 80% of Caribbean internet users browse on their phones. If your website isn't optimised for mobile — if the text is too small, the buttons are hard to tap, or the layout breaks on a small screen — you're losing the majority of your visitors before they even read a word.


How to check: Open your website on your phone right now. Can you read the text without zooming in? Can you tap the buttons easily? Does the layout look intentional — or like a desktop site squeezed onto a small screen?


The fix: A mobile-first website design that looks and works perfectly on every screen size. Every BluWav website is built mobile-first — because that's where your customers are.

02

It Takes Too Long to Load

Caribbean internet speeds vary significantly. A website that loads in 2 seconds in Port of Spain might take 8 seconds in a rural area. Research shows that 53% of mobile users abandon a website that takes more than 3 seconds to load.


Every second of load time costs you customers. And slow websites also rank lower on Google — so you're losing visibility as well as conversions.


How to check: Go to PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev) and enter your website URL. Google will give you a score and tell you exactly what's slowing your site down.


The fix: Optimised images, clean code, fast hosting, and a content delivery network (CDN). A well-built website should load in under 2 seconds on a mobile connection.

03

There's No Clear Call to Action

A visitor lands on your website. They're interested. They read a bit. And then... they don't know what to do next. There's no obvious button to call you, no WhatsApp link, no booking form, no clear next step.


So they leave. Not because they weren't interested — but because you didn't make it easy enough for them to take action.


How to check: Look at your homepage. Within 5 seconds, can a visitor clearly see how to contact you or take the next step? Is there a prominent phone number, WhatsApp button, or booking form?


The fix: Every page of your website should have a clear, prominent call to action — a button that tells visitors exactly what to do next. "Call Now," "WhatsApp Us," "Book a Consultation," "Get a Quote." Make it impossible to miss.

04

The Design Looks Outdated

First impressions happen in milliseconds. Research shows that users form an opinion about a website in 0.05 seconds — before they've read a single word. If your website looks like it was built in 2010, customers assume your business is behind the times.


In the Caribbean, where trust is everything, an outdated website can be the difference between a customer choosing you or your competitor. It signals that you don't invest in your business — so why would they invest in you?


How to check: Look at your website honestly. Does it look modern and professional? Does it look like the websites of businesses you admire? Or does it look dated, cluttered, or amateur?


The fix: A clean, modern design with professional photography, consistent branding, and a layout that guides visitors through your content naturally. You don't need to spend a fortune — you need to spend it wisely.

05

Google Can't Find It

You have a website. But when someone searches for what you offer in your area, your website doesn't appear. This is an SEO problem — and it's extremely common among Caribbean businesses.


A website that Google can't find is like a shop with no sign on the street. It exists — but no one knows it's there.


How to check: Search Google for your business type and location — for example, "plumber in San Fernando" or "catering company Kingston." Does your website appear? If not, you have an SEO problem.


The fix: On-page SEO essentials — proper page titles, meta descriptions, header tags, and keyword-optimised content. Google Search Console setup so you can monitor your performance. And a Google Business Profile that links to your website.

What a High-Converting Caribbean Business Website Looks Like

A website that actually brings in customers has these elements:

✅ The BluWav Website Checklist

  • Loads in under 2 seconds on mobile
  • Looks perfect on every screen size
  • Has a clear headline that explains what you do and who you serve
  • Has a prominent WhatsApp button and phone number above the fold
  • Has professional photos of your business, products, or services
  • Has customer testimonials or reviews
  • Has a clear call to action on every page
  • Has proper page titles and meta descriptions for SEO
  • Is connected to Google Search Console and Google Analytics
  • Has a Google Business Profile that links to it
  • Has an SSL certificate (the padlock in the browser bar)
  • Has a contact form or booking system
