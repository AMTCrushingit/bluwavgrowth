4. Payment Terms
  • Commission is earned when the client's payment is received and cleared by BluWav Growth.
  • Commission is paid within 7 business days of payment clearance.
  • CRM recurring commissions are paid monthly for the first 3 months following the client's first payment.
  • Minimum payout threshold: $50 USD equivalent.
  • If a client cancels or requests a full refund within 14 days of signing, the commission for that deal is reversed.
  • Payment will be made via bank transfer, PayPal, or WiPay as agreed.
  • All commissions are paid in USD.
  • The Sales Agent is responsible for any applicable taxes on commission income.
  • No commission is payable on setup or onboarding fees.
5. Sales Agent Responsibilities

The Sales Agent agrees to:

  • Represent BluWav Growth honestly and professionally at all times.
  • Use only approved pitch materials, pricing, and service descriptions.
  • Submit all leads through the agreed tracking method (WhatsApp, email, or CRM).
  • Not make promises or guarantees to clients beyond what is stated in official materials.
  • Not misrepresent timelines, pricing, or deliverables.
  • Complete the BluWav Growth onboarding session before actively selling.
  • Maintain confidentiality of all BluWav Growth business information, pricing strategies, client data, and sales material.
  • Position BluWav Growth as a digital growth systems firm, not merely a website agency or CRM provider.
6. What Is Not Commissionable
  • Existing clients upgrading without Sales Agent involvement.
  • Government or institutional contracts negotiated directly by BluWav Growth.
  • Clients sourced directly by BluWav Growth without Sales Agent involvement.
  • CRM Solo ($999 one-time) sales.
  • Setup or onboarding fees on any CRM package.
  • CRM recurring payments after the first 3 months.
7. Exclusivity and Territory

This Agreement is non-exclusive. BluWav Growth may engage multiple Sales Agents simultaneously. The Sales Agent may not represent competing digital presence, web design, or CRM services during the term of this Agreement without prior written consent from BluWav Growth.

8. Confidentiality

The Sales Agent agrees to keep all BluWav Growth business information, pricing, client data, strategies, and sales materials strictly confidential. This obligation continues for 12 months after the termination of this Agreement.

9. Termination
  • Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice.
  • Upon termination, earned commissions on closed deals will be paid within 30 days.
  • CRM recurring commissions will continue for the agreed 3-month duration on deals closed before termination.
  • The Sales Agent must immediately cease representing BluWav Growth upon termination.
  • The Sales Agent must return and/or destroy all BluWav Growth documents, promotional material, and assets.
10. General Terms
  • Any disputes will first be addressed through good-faith negotiation between the parties.
  • This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter herein.
  • Amendments must be agreed in writing and signed by both parties.
