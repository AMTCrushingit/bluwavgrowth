Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

BluWav Caribbean uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website performance, understand visitor behavior, and enhance your experience. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding their use.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences, improve functionality, and provide insights into how visitors interact with content.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure our website functions properly
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Understand website traffic and visitor behavior
  • Measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns
  • Remember preferences and settings

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled. Examples include:

  • Security features
  • Form submissions
  • Session management
  • Website functionality
Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website. Examples may include:

  • Google Analytics
  • Visitor behavior tracking
  • Website performance monitoring

Information collected is aggregated and does not directly identify individual visitors.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies help us understand the effectiveness of advertising and marketing initiatives. Examples may include:

  • Conversion tracking
  • Campaign performance measurement
  • Retargeting technologies

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to view, delete, block, or adjust cookie preferences. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

You can also manage your preferences using our cookie consent tool, which appears when you first visit our website.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services such as Google Analytics, scheduling tools, CRM platforms, and marketing tools. These providers may place cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, regulations, or our services. Any updates will be posted on this page.
