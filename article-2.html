Google Business Profile: The Free Tool Every Caribbean MSME Needs Right Now

It's free. It takes 30 minutes to set up. And it could be the single most impactful thing you do for your business this year.

BluWav Growth·4 min read·June 28, 2026

Not on Google Business Profile? You are invisible to thousands of customers every day. The good news: it is free and takes 30 minutes to set up. Here is how.

Most Caribbean business owners have heard of it. Far fewer have actually done it properly. And almost none are using it to its full potential.

That's a massive opportunity, because the businesses that get this right are the ones showing up at the top of Google when customers search for exactly what you offer.

📍 What is Google Business Profile? It's the listing that appears when someone searches for your business on Google, or searches for businesses like yours in your area. It shows your name, address, phone number, hours, photos, reviews, and a link to your website. It's what puts you on Google Maps.

Why Google Business Profile Matters for Caribbean Businesses

Here's the reality of how Caribbean customers find businesses today:

  • They open Google on their phone
  • They type something like "plumber near me" or "best salon in San Fernando"
  • They look at the first 3 results that appear, usually a map with business listings
  • They click on one, check the reviews and photos, and call

If your business doesn't appear in those first 3 results, you don't exist to that customer. They'll call someone else. And they'll never know you were an option.

Google Business Profile is what gets you into those results. And the best part? It's completely free.

What a Complete Google Business Profile Looks Like

Most Caribbean businesses that have a Google Business Profile have an incomplete one. Here's what a fully optimised profile includes, and why each element matters:

Business Name, Address, and Phone Number

This sounds obvious, but it needs to be exactly consistent across every platform, your website, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Google. Inconsistencies confuse Google and hurt your ranking.

Business Category

Choose the most specific category that describes your business. "Restaurant" is less effective than "Caribbean Restaurant" or "Trinidadian Restaurant." The more specific, the better Google can match you to relevant searches.

Opening Hours

Include your regular hours AND special hours for public holidays. Nothing frustrates a customer more than showing up to a business that Google says is open, and finding it closed.

Photos

Businesses with photos receive 42% more requests for directions and 35% more website clicks than those without. Add photos of your storefront, your products or services, your team, and your work. Update them regularly.

Customer Reviews

Reviews are the single most powerful trust signal on Google. Businesses with more positive reviews rank higher and convert more customers. We'll cover how to get more reviews in a moment.

Business Description

Write a clear, keyword-rich description of what you do and who you serve. Include the areas you serve and the specific services you offer. This helps Google understand your business and match you to relevant searches.

WhatsApp and Website Links

Add your WhatsApp number and website URL. Caribbean customers want to WhatsApp, make it as easy as possible for them to reach you.

How to Set Up Your Google Business Profile in 30 Minutes

1

Go to business.google.com

Sign in with your Google account (or create one if you don't have one). Click "Add your business to Google."

2

Enter your business name

Type your exact business name. If it already exists, claim it. If not, create a new listing.

3

Choose your business category

Select the most specific category that describes your business. You can add additional categories later.

4

Add your location

If customers visit your location, add your address. If you serve customers at their location (like a plumber or caterer), you can add a service area instead.

5

Add your contact details

Add your phone number, WhatsApp number, and website URL. Make sure these match exactly what's on your website.

6

Verify your business

Google will ask you to verify your business, usually via a phone call, text message, or postcard. Complete this step, unverified listings don't appear in search results.

7

Complete your profile

Add your hours, photos, business description, and any other relevant information. The more complete your profile, the better you'll rank.

How to Get More Google Reviews, The Right Way

Reviews are the lifeblood of your Google Business Profile. Here's how to get more of them without violating Google's guidelines:

  • Ask every satisfied customer. After a successful job or service, simply ask: "Would you mind leaving us a Google review? It really helps our business." Most people are happy to help if you ask.
  • Make it easy. Create a short link to your Google review page and send it via WhatsApp. The fewer steps, the more reviews you'll get.
  • Respond to every review. Thank customers for positive reviews. Respond professionally to negative ones. This shows Google, and potential customers, that you're engaged and care about your service.
  • Never buy reviews. Google detects fake reviews and will penalise your listing. Only ask real customers for honest reviews.
⚠️ Important: Never offer incentives (discounts, free products) in exchange for reviews. This violates Google's policies and can get your listing suspended.

Common Google Business Profile Mistakes Caribbean Businesses Make

  • Setting it up and forgetting it. Google rewards active profiles. Post updates, add new photos, and respond to reviews regularly.
  • Inconsistent information. If your phone number on Google is different from your website, Google gets confused and your ranking suffers.
  • No photos. A profile with no photos looks abandoned. Add at least 10 high-quality photos when you set up your profile.
  • Ignoring the Q&A section. Customers can ask questions on your profile. Answer them promptly, or add your own frequently asked questions proactively.
  • Not using Google Posts. You can post updates, offers, and events directly on your Google Business Profile. These appear in search results and keep your profile fresh.

What Happens After You Set It Up

Once your Google Business Profile is complete and verified, you'll start appearing in local search results and on Google Maps. But the work doesn't stop there.

The businesses that dominate local search are the ones that treat their Google Business Profile like a living, breathing part of their business, not a one-time setup task. They post regularly, respond to reviews, add new photos, and keep their information up to date.

🌊 BluWav tip: Set a reminder to check your Google Business Profile every two weeks. Respond to any new reviews, add a new photo, and post an update. This consistent activity signals to Google that your business is active, and rewards you with higher rankings.
