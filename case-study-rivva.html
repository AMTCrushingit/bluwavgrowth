The Brief

The client came with a concept and a clear vision: a service marketplace built specifically for the Caribbean. The goal was to connect verified local professionals — electricians, photographers, caterers, developers, plumbers — with clients who needed them, across multiple islands, with a trust layer that made every transaction feel safe and credible.

The platform needed to feel unmistakably Caribbean, work on low-end phones, and be ready to generate revenue from day one. BluWav was brought in to take the concept from zero to a fully deployed, production-ready platform.

The Challenge

No Caribbean-native service marketplace existed. The market was fragmented, informal, and trust-deficient. Skilled professionals had no digital presence. Clients had no reliable way to find and vet them.

The platform needed to solve three problems simultaneously: give providers a credible digital identity, give clients a trustworthy way to find and hire, and give the platform itself a revenue model that worked from launch.

It also needed to work across six different island markets, each with their own service norms, payment preferences, and connectivity realities.

🌎

What Was Built

A complete marketplace platform with three distinct user flows across 22 pages and routes, deployed to production on Vercel.

🎯

Matching Engine

Island-first, category-matched, verification-gated. Providers ranked by rating, response speed, and completed jobs. Clients get the right professional for their island and their need.

✅

Credii Trust Score

Providers are scored on identity verification, skill certification, response speed, and client reviews. Every verified provider carries a Credii badge that clients can trust.

📍

Service Area Targeting

Providers specify the exact districts and communities they serve. Clients search by island and location. The platform routes jobs to the right professionals, not just the nearest ones.

⚡

Live Activity Feed

Real-time job postings and provider activity signal marketplace energy from day one. Visitors see a living platform, not an empty directory.

💳

Stripe Subscriptions

Three-tier provider subscriptions with Stripe billing, fully integrated. Revenue generation is built in from launch, not bolted on later.

📊

Admin and Analytics Panel

Provider approvals, category management, platform analytics, and grant-ready data exports. The operator has full visibility and control from day one.

Three Distinct User Flows

Every user type has a purpose-built experience across 22 pages and routes.

👤

Client Flow

Search by island, category, and location. Browse verified providers with trust scores and reviews. Post a job and receive matched proposals. Book and pay securely.

🔧

Provider Flow

Create a verified profile with Credii credentials. Set service areas by district and community. Receive matched job leads. Manage proposals, bookings, and reviews from a dedicated dashboard.

🛠

Admin Flow

Approve providers, manage categories, monitor platform health, and export grant-ready data. Full operational control from a dedicated admin and Credii panel.
