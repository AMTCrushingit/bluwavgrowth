Key Responsibilities
  • Manage and grow social media presence across all three brands (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn)
  • Create and schedule engaging content, posts, stories, reels, and short-form video

  • Support SEO content updates on bluwavgrowth.com and group websites

Requirements
  • 2+ years experience in digital marketing, social media management, or content creation
  • Strong writing skills, clear, engaging, and on-brand across different voices
  • Proficient in Canva, Meta Business Suite, and scheduling tools (Buffer, Later, or similar)
  • Understanding of SEO basics and Google Analytics
  • Organised, self-directed, and able to manage multiple brands simultaneously
  • Caribbean-based, understanding of local business culture is essential
  • Experience with email marketing platforms (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or similar) is a plus
