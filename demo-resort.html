About the Resort

Where Jamaica
Comes Alive.

Sunnyside Cove isn't just a resort, it's a feeling. Perched on the edge of Negril's legendary Seven Mile Beach, our 48-acre property is a celebration of everything that makes Jamaica extraordinary: the warmth of the people, the rhythm of the music, the colour of the sunsets.

From our clifftop infinity pool to our open-air reggae stage, every corner of Sunnyside Cove is designed to make you feel the island in your bones. All-inclusive means all the jerk chicken, rum punch, and snorkelling your heart desires.

Island Life

Experiences That Move You

🎵
Reggae Nights

Live bands every evening on our open-air stage. Dance barefoot under the stars to the rhythm of Jamaica.

🤿
Reef Snorkelling

Explore vibrant coral gardens teeming with tropical fish just steps from our beach. Equipment included.

🍳
Jerk & Rum Classes

Learn to cook authentic Jamaican jerk chicken and craft the perfect rum punch with our local chefs.

🌅
Sunset Catamaran

Sail into the golden hour on our private catamaran. Champagne, snacks, and the most beautiful sunset on earth.

💆
Spa & Wellness

Balinese-inspired treatments using local botanicals. Massages, facials, and yoga on the beach at sunrise.

🏄
Water Sports

Paddleboarding, kayaking, windsurfing, and jet skiing, all included in your stay. No extra charge.

🚌
Island Excursions

Dunn's River Falls, Blue Lagoon, YS Falls, our guides take you to Jamaica's most breathtaking spots.

🌿
Blue Mountain Hike

Trek through Jamaica's legendary Blue Mountains at dawn and taste the world's finest coffee at the source.
