FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really own everything?+

Yes. Your website, CRM, automations, content, and accounts are all yours forever. BluWav does not lock you into monthly subscriptions or proprietary systems.

Is there a monthly fee?+

Only the Surge plan includes a monthly component because it comes with advanced automation, reporting, and a dedicated account manager. Ripple and Current are one-time setup fees — no monthly subscription required.

What's included in every plan?+

Every BluWav Growth System includes the essential tools your business needs to get found online, be chosen first, and grow… powered by AI and built for Caribbean MSMEs. This includes your website, Google Business Profile setup, CRM, basic automation, analytics, SEO essentials, and WhatsApp chat integration.

How fast will my system be ready?+

Most businesses go live in 48–72 hours, depending on how quickly you provide your content and business details.

What is the CRM and how does it help me?+

The CRM helps you track leads, manage customers, send automated messages, and stay organized. It's included in every plan — with no monthly fee — and is fully integrated with your website and WhatsApp.

Do you manage my Google or Bing ads?+

No. We set up your accounts, tracking, and ready-to-use campaign templates in the Surge plan. You can run ads yourself or hire someone later — you're not locked in.

What's the difference between the plans?+

Ripple gives you the essentials to get online and get found.

Current adds visibility tools, WhatsApp setup, reviews, and local SEO.

Surge adds multi-search engine visibility, ad-ready setup, advanced automation, and a dedicated account manager.

Do you write my content?+

Yes. We provide starter content for your website, Google Business Profile, and WhatsApp. You can edit it anytime.

What do I need to get started?+

Just your business name, logo (if you have one), contact details, and a short description of what you offer. We handle everything else.

Can I upgrade later?+

Yes. You can start with Ripple or Current and upgrade to Surge anytime. Your existing setup carries over — without you losing anything.

What support do I get?+

Ripple: Standard support

Current: Priority support

Surge: Dedicated account manager

Do you work with businesses outside the Caribbean?+

Yes, BluWav works globally. But our systems are especially optimized for Caribbean MSMEs and regional search behavior.
