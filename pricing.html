CRM — Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?+

No. Your 7-day trial is completely free and does not require a credit card.

What happens after my 7-day trial ends?+

You can continue using BluWav CRM for $29 USD/month. A $99 one-time setup fee will be applied at that time.

Is the setup fee required for everyone?+

The setup fee applies to standalone CRM users. It is included free with any BluWav Growth package.

What does the setup fee cover?+
Your $99 setup fee covers:
– CRM configuration
– Data import (if needed)
– Custom fields
– Pipeline setup
– WhatsApp automation setup
– Onboarding support
Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. There are no contracts and no long-term commitments.

Is BluWav CRM built for Caribbean businesses?+

Yes. BluWav CRM is designed specifically for how Caribbean MSMEs operate — fast, simple, mobile-friendly, and built for real-world workflows.

Growth Systems — Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is delivery?+

Websites are delivered within 48 hours of project start. CRM setup is completed within the same window for Growth and Surge packages.

Do I own my website?+

Yes. You own everything forever. A signed agreement is included with every project — your website, your content, your business.

What support is included?+

Ripple includes basic support. Current includes 14-day post-launch support. Surge includes priority support and quarterly audits.

Is CRM included in Growth packages?+

Yes. BluWav Current and Surge both include CRM setup. The $99 setup fee is waived for all Growth package clients.

Can I upgrade my package later?+

Absolutely. You can start with Ripple and upgrade to Current or Surge at any time. We make the transition seamless.
