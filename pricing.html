FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really own everything?+

Yes. Your website, CRM, automations, content, and accounts are all yours forever. BluWav does not lock you into monthly subscriptions or proprietary systems. You own it, full stop.

Is there a monthly fee?+

Your Growth System package is a one-time setup fee. Hosting is included for 6 months, then $200 every 6 months for secure hosting, SSL, updates, and support. The CRM is separate. CRM Starter is $99 onboarding + $59/month. CRM Lite is $199 onboarding + $99/month (or $990/year). CRM Premium is $299 onboarding + $179/month (or $1,990/year). Enterprise is $599 onboarding + $499/month. White-Label is $699 onboarding + $697/month (annual contract). A 14-day free trial is included with every plan so you can experience the CRM before choosing.

What's included in every plan?+

Every BluWav Growth System includes: a modern website you own forever, Google Business Profile setup, CRM (14-day trial included), smart lead automation, a performance dashboard, on-page SEO essentials, Google Search Console + Analytics 4 setup, WhatsApp chat button, mobile-optimized design, and fast secure hosting. All built for modern businesses.

How fast will my system be ready?+

Most businesses go live in 48–72 hours from the moment you provide your content and business details. We move fast, that's the BluWav promise.

What is the CRM and how does it help me?+

The CRM is your customer management hub, track leads, manage contacts, automate follow-ups, and monitor your pipeline all in one place. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial of CRM Premium so you can experience the full system. After your trial, choose CRM Starter ($99 onboarding + $59/month) for the basics, CRM Lite ($199 onboarding + $99/month) for more flexibility, CRM Premium ($299 onboarding + $179/month) for unlimited contacts, automation, team access, and all future upgrades, or Enterprise ($599 onboarding + $499/month) for larger teams needing custom workflows and dedicated support.

What's the difference between the three plans?+

Ripple ($1,800), The fastest way to get online. Website, Google visibility, CRM trial, smart automation, and performance dashboard. Perfect for businesses starting their digital journey.

Current ($3,000), The complete growth engine. Everything in Ripple plus WhatsApp Business setup, advanced lead sequences, local SEO + competitor analysis, reputation builder, automated lead pipeline, a conversion landing page, and a monthly check-in call. Best for businesses ready to grow consistently.

Surge ($5,000+), Full multi-channel domination. Everything in Current plus Google Ads campaign setup, multi-search engine listing, full WhatsApp sales funnel, monthly visibility reports, quarterly strategy reviews, and a dedicated account manager. For businesses scaling aggressively.

Do you run my Google Ads?+

For Ripple and Current, we set up your Google Analytics, Search Console, and tracking, but ad management is not included. For Surge, we set up and launch your Google Ads campaign and provide the ad-ready infrastructure. You control the ad budget. If you want ongoing ad management after launch, we can discuss that separately.

What is the CRM trial and how does it work?+

Every BluWav Growth plan includes a 14-day free trial of CRM Premium, full access, no credit card required. You'll experience unlimited contacts, pipeline tracking, automated follow-ups, WhatsApp integration, and team features. After your trial, choose CRM Starter ($99 onboarding + $59/month), CRM Lite ($199 onboarding + $99/month), CRM Premium ($299 onboarding + $179/month), or Enterprise ($599 onboarding + $499/month) for the full experience.

Do you offer a nonprofit rate?+

Yes. Registered nonprofits qualify for CRM Premium at $99/month (no setup fee), our community rate. Email hello@bluwavgrowth.com with your nonprofit registration number and we'll set it up.

What about the White-Label option?+

The White-Label plan ($697/month + $699 onboarding, annual contract) lets agencies and consultants resell BluWav CRM under their own brand. Your logo, your domain, clients never see BluWav. You manage unlimited client accounts from one Super Admin dashboard and keep the margin. Book a demo call to learn more.

Do you write my content?+

Yes. We provide starter content for your website, Google Business Profile, and WhatsApp. It's written for your business type and location. You can edit it anytime, you own it.

What do I need to get started?+

Just your business name, logo (if you have one), contact details, and a short description of what you offer. We handle everything else, design, setup, content, and launch.

Can I upgrade later?+

Yes. You can start with Ripple or Current and upgrade to Surge anytime. Your existing website, CRM data, and setup carry over, you never lose anything. Same for the CRM: start on Lite and upgrade to Premium whenever you're ready.

What support do I get?+

Ripple: Standard support, email response within 24 hours.

Current: Priority support + monthly check-in call.

Surge: Dedicated account manager + quarterly strategy review.

Do you work with businesses in my country?
Yes. BluWav Growth works with businesses worldwide. Our systems are built for any market, any country, any industry. We deliver in 48 hours and you own everything we build.+

Yes. BluWav works with businesses globally. Our systems are especially optimised for modern businesses and regional search behaviour, but the tools work anywhere.
