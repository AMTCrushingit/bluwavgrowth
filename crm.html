Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?+

No. Your 14-day trial is completely free and does not require a credit card. Full access to CRM Lite from day one.

What happens after my trial ends?+

You choose your plan: CRM Lite at $199 onboarding + $99/month or CRM Premium at $299 onboarding + $179/month. Both include a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. Your data is preserved regardless of which plan you choose.one-time onboarding fee applies to all plans. Your data remains accessible throughout your 14-day trial.

Do I lose my data if I don't upgrade?+

No. Your data remains accessible for 14 days after trial expiration, giving you time to export or decide on a plan.

Do Lite users get updates?+

CRM Lite users receive bug fixes and security patches only. All new features, upgrades, modules, dashboards, and automation templates are exclusive to CRM Premium subscribers.

Is BluWav CRM built for Caribbean businesses?+

Yes — every feature is designed for Caribbean MSMEs, WhatsApp-first workflows, and regional business needs. Built by the BluWav team in Trinidad & Tobago.

Can I get CRM without a BluWav Growth package?+

Yes. Standalone CRM pricing is available: CRM Lite at $199 setup + $99/month (or $990/year), or CRM Premium at $299 setup + $179/month (or $1,790/year). Both include the same 14-day free trial.
