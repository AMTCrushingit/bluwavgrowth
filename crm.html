Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?+

No. Your 30-day trial is completely free and does not require a credit card. Full access to CRM Lite from day one.

What happens after my trial ends?+

You choose your plan: CRM Lite at $799 (trial upgrade price, saving $100) or CRM Premium with the $299 installation fee waived. If you don't upgrade, your data remains accessible for 30 days after trial expiration.

Do I lose my data if I don't upgrade?+

No. Your data remains accessible for 30 days after trial expiration, giving you time to export or decide on a plan.

Do Lite users get updates?+

CRM Lite users receive bug fixes and security patches only. All new features, upgrades, modules, dashboards, and automation templates are exclusive to CRM Premium subscribers.

Is BluWav CRM built for Caribbean businesses?+

Yes — every feature is designed for Caribbean MSMEs, WhatsApp-first workflows, and regional business needs. Built by the BluWav team in Trinidad & Tobago.

Can I get CRM without a BluWav Growth package?+

Yes. Standalone CRM pricing is available: CRM Lite at $999 one-time, or CRM Premium at $299 installation + $79/month. Both include the same 30-day free trial.
