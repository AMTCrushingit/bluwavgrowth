Transformation 01

From Generic to Authoritative

The Challenge

The old AADEK site had no brand voice, no positioning, and no personality. It looked like every other generic contractor website, indistinguishable from a one-person operation with no credentials.

For a firm led by a dual-licensed Professional Engineer with 20+ years of Caribbean project experience, this was a critical missed opportunity.

Old Homepage Headline
"You will find the latest information about our company here."

The Solution

BluWav rebuilt AADEK's brand voice from the ground up, authoritative, technical, and precise. Every word was written to reflect the calibre of engineering Karen delivers.

New Homepage Headline
"Engineering Excellence. Precisely Delivered."
Brand Positioning Delivered
  • Dark, premium engineering aesthetic, navy, gold, steel palette
  • Playfair Display headlines, gravitas and authority
  • Blueprint grid texture, engineering identity
  • Karen Myers-Rolle, PE positioned as the authority
Transformation 02

Three Divisions. Three Revenue Streams.

The Challenge

AADEK operates three distinct businesses, AADEK Consulting, Balanced as Designed, and Adrian's Safe Air & Mechanical. The old site buried all three under a single confusing navigation with no clear differentiation.

Potential clients couldn't tell what AADEK actually did, let alone which division to engage for their specific need.

The Solution

Each division was given its own identity, colour accent, service list, and value proposition, presented as a unified group with three specialist arms.

📐 AADEK Consulting
Mechanical Engineering Consultancy, 9 services, gold accent
⚖️ Balanced as Designed
Testing, Adjusting & Balancing, 8 services, cyan accent
🔧 Adrian's Safe Air & Mechanical
Installation & Maintenance, 8 services, steel accent
Transformation 03

Making the Engineer the Authority

The Challenge

Karen Myers-Rolle holds dual Professional Engineer licenses, one of the most significant credentials in the engineering profession. The old site made no mention of her name, her qualifications, or her experience.

In a trust-driven industry like engineering consultancy, the principal's credentials are the single most powerful conversion tool. They were completely absent.

The Solution

Karen was positioned as the centrepiece of the site, her dual PE licensure, Caribbean expertise, and 20+ years of experience featured prominently throughout.

Credibility Architecture Built
  • Dual PE badge in hero card and footer
  • Dedicated About section with professional bio
  • 4 credential badges: PE, Mechanical Specialist, Caribbean Experience, Founder
  • Stats: 20+ years, 3 divisions, 100+ projects, 2x PE
  • "Karen Myers-Rolle, PE" in page title and meta tags for SEO
