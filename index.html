Frequently Asked Questions

Every BluWav membership includes a professional framework-based business website personalized with your branding, content, services and contact details, along with domain registration, hosting, SSL security, professional business email, mobile-responsive design, contact form, WhatsApp click-to-chat, basic on-page search setup, search engine indexing, and ongoing technical maintenance. Higher plans add Google and LinkedIn visibility, social media posts, lead capture, and more.
All BluWav membership plans require an initial 12-month commitment. This allows us to build, optimize, and grow your digital presence properly. After the initial term, your membership continues on a monthly basis.
The enrollment fee covers the setup, onboarding, and initial build of your digital presence. It is a one-time fee paid at the start of your membership. Launch is US$199, Presence is US$249, Growth is US$299, and Pro is US$399.
BluWav websites are built using proven professional frameworks and personalized with your logo, colours, content, services, images and contact details. They are not bespoke custom-coded websites. Requirements such as e-commerce, booking platforms, membership portals, or complex integrations require a separate quotation.
BluWav serves businesses across the Caribbean including Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, Jamaica, the Bahamas, Guyana, Saint Lucia, Grenada, Antigua and Barbuda, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, and other Caribbean territories. If you are unsure whether we serve your country, request a free review and we will confirm.
BluWav membership plans do not include a CRM. As your business grows, BluWav clients can access StratiixOne, a separate business management platform with its own 14-day free trial. StratiixOne is not part of any BluWav membership package.
