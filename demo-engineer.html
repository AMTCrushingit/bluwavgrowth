Our Founder

Monique Adderley, P.Eng, is the founder and principal engineer of Adderley & Associates Engineering Group, a firm born from a deep love of the Bahamas and a commitment to building infrastructure that lasts.

A proud Bahamian, Monique holds Professional Engineer licensure in both Florida and Texas, bringing international standards to every project she leads. From government ministries in Nassau to luxury resort developments on private cays, her work shapes the built environment of the islands she calls home.
