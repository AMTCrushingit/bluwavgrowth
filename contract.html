Client Agreement

BluWav Growth
Standard Client Agreement

Plain English. No legal jargon. This is what we agree to before any work begins.

← Go Back

📋 This agreement applies to all BluWav Growth packages — Ripple, Current, Surge, and all CRM plans (Starter, Lite, Premium, Enterprise, and White-Label). A personalised version with your specific package details and pricing will be sent to you via email before any work begins.

1 What BluWav Growth Agrees to Do

BluWav Growth agrees to deliver the services outlined in your chosen package within the agreed timeframe (typically 48–72 hours from payment confirmation). Specifically, we agree to:

  • Deliver all agreed services as described in your package
  • Communicate with you via email throughout the project
  • Provide you with a preview before going live — nothing goes live without your approval
  • Transfer full ownership of all deliverables to you upon payment
  • Keep your business information strictly confidential
  • Provide 30 days of post-launch support for any bugs or issues

2 What You Agree to Do

To ensure we can deliver on time, you agree to:

  • Provide all required content (logo, photos, text, business details) within 48 hours of project start
  • Respond to emails within 24 hours during the project
  • Provide feedback and approval within 48 hours of receiving a preview
  • Make payment as agreed before work begins (or as per the agreed payment schedule)
  • Not share login credentials or access details with third parties without notifying us

3 Ownership — You Own Everything

🔒 Once payment is complete, you own 100% of everything we build for you — your website, your domain, your CRM data, your content. No lock-in. No hidden fees. Any ongoing CRM subscription is entirely your choice and can be cancelled anytime. Ever.

This includes:

  • Your website files and code
  • Your domain name (registered in your name)
  • Your Google Business Profile
  • Your WhatsApp Business account
  • All CRM data and contacts
  • All content created for your project

4 Payment Terms

Payment is due before work begins unless a split payment schedule has been agreed in writing via email.

  • Accepted methods: WiPay, PayPal, bank transfer, Linx, and other methods confirmed at time of invoice
  • Split payments: Available on Current and Surge packages — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
  • CRM Starter: $99 one-time onboarding fee, then $59/month billed monthly
  • CRM Lite: $199 one-time onboarding fee, then $99/month (or $990/year)
  • CRM Premium: $299 one-time onboarding fee, then $179/month (or $1,990/year) — introductory rate, locks in for life
  • CRM Enterprise: $599 white-glove onboarding fee, then $499/month
  • White-Label CRM: $699 one-time onboarding fee, then $697/month (annual contract)
  • Nonprofit rate: CRM Premium at $99/month with no setup fee — proof of registered nonprofit status required
  • Refunds: If we fail to deliver within the agreed timeframe with no valid reason, you are entitled to a full refund
  • Cancellation: If you cancel after work has begun, a fee proportional to work completed will apply

5 Hosting & Ongoing Services

  • Hosting is included for 6 months from your go-live date
  • After 6 months, hosting renews at $200 every 6 months (includes SSL, security updates, and support)
  • You may move your website to any other host at any time — we will provide all files
  • CRM subscriptions (Starter $59/mo, Lite $99/mo, Premium $179/mo, Enterprise $499/mo) can be cancelled with 30 days written notice via email
  • White-Label CRM operates on an annual contract — cancellation terms are outlined in the separate White-Label Partner Agreement

6 Revisions & Changes

  • Each package includes up to 2 rounds of revisions before go-live
  • Additional revisions after go-live are billed at $75/hour
  • Major scope changes (e.g. adding pages, new features) will be quoted separately
  • Minor text/image updates within 14 days of launch are included at no charge

7 Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all business information, pricing, and project details confidential. BluWav Growth will never share your business data, client lists, or login credentials with any third party without your written consent.

8 Limitation of Liability

BluWav Growth's liability is limited to the amount paid for the specific service in question. We are not liable for indirect losses such as lost revenue or business opportunities. We will always act in good faith and communicate openly if any issues arise.

9 Dispute Resolution

If a dispute arises, both parties agree to first attempt resolution via email within 14 days. If unresolved, disputes will be handled in good faith under the applicable laws of the jurisdiction in which BluWav Growth operates.

10 Agreement

By submitting your application and making payment, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to these terms. A personalised version of this agreement — with your specific package details, pricing, and timeline — will be sent to you via email before any work begins.
