1. Services

ClientFlow Caribbean will provide the services included in the selected package. The specific deliverables and timeline will be confirmed in writing before work begins.

2. Payment Terms

Pay in Full: Full payment upfront. Project starts within 24 hours.

Pay in 2: 50% deposit to start. 50% due on delivery and approval.

Pay in 3: 40% deposit, 30% at midpoint, 30% on final delivery. Available for Grow and Flourish packages only.

Deposits are non-refundable once work has commenced. Final deliverables will not be released until all payments are received in full.

3. Timeline & Delivery

ClientFlow Start: 48-72 hours. ClientFlow Grow: 3-5 business days. ClientFlow Flourish: 7-14 business days for setup, then ongoing monthly. Timelines depend on the Client providing content and approvals promptly.

4. Revisions

Start: 1 revision round. Grow: 2 revision rounds. Flourish: 3 revision rounds for initial setup. Additional revisions are available at an agreed rate. Major scope changes will be quoted separately.

5. Ownership

Upon full payment, the Client owns all deliverables including website files, domain, and hosting. ClientFlow Caribbean retains the right to display completed work in its portfolio unless the Client requests otherwise in writing.

6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all shared business information confidential. ClientFlow Caribbean will not share Client data with third parties without written consent.

7. Limitation of Liability

ClientFlow Caribbean does not guarantee specific search rankings, lead volumes, or revenue results. Total liability is limited to fees paid for the specific service in question.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate with written notice. The Client pays for all work completed. Deposits are non-refundable once work has started. Monthly retainers require 30 days written notice to terminate.

9. Dispute Resolution

Disputes will first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Trinidad and Tobago.

10. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to provide all required content within 48 hours of project start, respond to review requests within 3 business days, and ensure all provided content is accurate and lawful.
