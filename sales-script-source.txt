Presenting BluWav Growth Systems
Only present solutions after you've listened. Tie every BluWav capability directly to something the
prospect just told you. Avoid feature-dumping — lead with outcomes. The Health Check is always the
next step, not a full pitch.

The One System, Every Channel Message
Use this framing when they ask what BluWav does:
LEAH: What BluWav does is bring everything together into one system — your website, your Google
listing, WhatsApp, your CRM, and automation — so they all work together to bring you more customers.
Right now, most businesses have bits and pieces that don't talk to each other. We fix that. And every
plan includes a CRM Lite Trial so you can see the automation working from day one.

Closing Techniques
The close for this call is simple: get the Health Check form completed — either together on the call right
now, or by sending them the link. No scheduling, no meeting. It takes two minutes.

Assumptive Close — Do It Now Together
LEAH: Brilliant — let's do it right now, it only takes two minutes. I'll ask you the four questions and fill
it in as we go. First one: what's your business name and what do you do?
💡 This is the strongest close — completing the form together on the call locks in engagement immediately and
means you control the quality of the answers. Move straight into the questions without waiting.

Send the Link Close — If They Prefer to Do It Themselves
LEAH: No problem at all — I'll send you the link right now by WhatsApp. It's just four questions, takes
about two minutes. Once you submit it, we'll analyse your online presence and come back to you with
the results. What's the best number to send it to?

Follow-Up Language
Most conversions happen on the follow-up. Be persistent without being pushy. Always give a reason for
calling back.

Same-Day Follow-Up (After Sending Info)
LEAH: Hi [Name], Leah from BluWav Growth here. I sent over the info and the Health Check link earlier
today — just wanted to make sure it landed okay. It's only four questions, takes two minutes. Did you
get a chance to have a look?
💡 If they haven't filled it in yet, offer to do it together right now on the call. 'I can walk you through it now if
you like — takes two minutes.'

48-Hour Follow-Up (Form Not Submitted)
LEAH: Hi [Name], Leah from BluWav Growth again. I know you're busy so I'll keep this quick — I just
wanted to follow up on the free Health Check link I sent. It's just four questions and we do all the
analysis for you — results back within 48 hours. Want me to walk you through it now? Takes two
minutes.

Voicemail Script
Keep voicemails under 30 seconds. Create curiosity, not information overload. Always end with a clear
call to action.

Voicemail — First Attempt
LEAH: Hi [Name], this is Leah calling from BluWav Growth. I'm reaching out because we're offering
local businesses a completely free Online Health Check — just four quick questions, and it shows you
exactly why customers might be choosing a competitor right now instead of you. No meeting, no
commitment, results in 48 hours. I'll send you the link on WhatsApp as well. Feel free to call me back on
[your number]. Have a great day!

Voicemail — Second Attempt
LEAH: Hi [Name], Leah from BluWav Growth again. I don't want to keep bothering you, so this will be
my last message for now. I sent the free Health Check link on WhatsApp — it's just four questions and
takes two minutes. A lot of businesses in [area/industry] have been really surprised by what we find. If
you have any questions, just message me back. Hope to speak soon!

Voicemail Tip
Smile while you record — it genuinely changes your tone. Speak at 80% of your normal pace. Say your
phone number slowly and repeat it once. Mention WhatsApp — it's the preferred channel for many
Caribbean business owners.
