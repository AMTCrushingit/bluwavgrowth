Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At BluWav Caribbean, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. We believe trust matters. Any information you share with BluWav is used to support our relationship with you and help us deliver better services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Website address
  • Company information
  • Information submitted through forms, reviews, assessments, or consultations

We may also collect limited technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Website usage data
  • Pages visited

How We Use Your Information

We use information collected to:

  • Respond to enquiries

Marketing Communications

From time to time, we may send updates, resources, promotions, or business-related information. You may unsubscribe at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions included in our communications.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers to help deliver our services, including:

These providers only receive information necessary to perform their services.

Data Security

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To make a request, please contact us using the information below.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, meet legal requirements, and support legitimate business operations.
